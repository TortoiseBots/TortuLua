src/*.c and src/*.h are the files of the tarball's src/ folder (lua.c and luac.c included, never built); doc/readme.html
and README.lua are the tarball's doc/readme.html and README.
Patches (security review R1, 2026-11-20; applied to dep/lua/src, kept as diffs against the tarball):
  patches/0001-match-step-limit.patch  lstrlib.c: a step counter shared by every scan the pattern functions make
  (string.find/match/gmatch/gsub abort with "pattern too complex" past a limit the engine sets per call from the call
  budget). match() charges one step per entry; the character class scan, singlematch, %b (matchbalance), the greedy and
  minimal expansions and a plain (non-pattern) find charge for every character they walk. The counter saturates instead
  of overflowing. The pattern functions are single C calls that the hook cannot interrupt; exponential backtracking, a
  1 MiB class scan or a 1 MiB plain find would otherwise hold the world thread for seconds.
Nothing else in dep/lua/src was edited. To check: unpack the tarball, apply the patch with `patch -p1`, diff against dep/lua.
